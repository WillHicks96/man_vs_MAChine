Overarching theme of this research is to explore a few relaxed/unrelaxed criteria for haloes and how they differentiate cluster scaling relations, radial profiles etc. We will do this in multiple steps. Start by downloading catalog from Frontier-E hydro simulation catalog for 10000 haloes, including the profiles. Within the profiles, let's look at 10 different types of profiles of 10 randomly chosen haloes. Choose the profiles that you think are relevent for this work.
